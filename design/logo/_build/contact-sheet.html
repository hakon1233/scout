Scout — logo concepts
Five distinct directions on the warm-paper brand. Founder's pick drops into the PER-219 header slot.

Dateline Wordmark
Type-first masthead. Says Scout is a publication, not an app — editorial, calm, confident. The red full-stop is the signal.
02
Trail Monogram
An 'S' drawn as a scouting trail that ends in a signal-red node — the scout's route to the one thing that matters. Strong app-icon.
03
Compass Star
A four-point navigation star, north in signal-red. The scout knows which way to look — orientation and editorial polish in one mark.
04
Signal Arcs
A wire-service transmission radiating from a single source. Scout goes out and brings back just the signal — literal and ownable.
05
Spyglass
The scout spots distant news; the lens catches the signal (red). Warm, human, unmistakably about finding what's out there.
