# A bedtime story for my kids (needs "make" mode; sample written by hand)

**Width:** one short story a night (about 400 words, 4 minutes aloud), written for the child's
name, age and interests, ending calm. Runs every evening at the reader's bedtime hour.

**Fits today?** No. It needs (1) a mode with no web sources and no news checks, (2) memory of
past stories so characters and titles do not repeat, (3) the child's details in the doc. See
`../beyond-news.md`, "Run-system changes".

**Why it is a starter:** kids' bedtime story apps already charge (PageeKids $44.99 a year,
Your AI Bedtime Stories $29.99 a year, Family Fable $49.99 a year, from App Store listings,
23 Nov 2026). A parent can write a doc in two minutes. Risk: a child's name in the doc.
Use a nickname; the doc goes into every run.

## Intent doc

```markdown
# Bedtime story for Mina

## Reader

A parent reading aloud to Mina, 5, at 19:30. Mina loves dinosaurs, the number 7 and her stuffed rabbit Pelle. Her brother Ola is 2 and appears sometimes.

## Report

- One new story, 350-450 words, a title and nothing else. A short quiet start, one small problem, a kind solution, a slow ending where everyone falls asleep
- Short sentences, read-aloud rhythm, a repeated line the child can say along (change it each story)
- Mina is the hero in about half the stories; in the rest she meets a new animal who needs help
- Every 7th story is about something real she did this week, if the reader added a line under "This week"
- Norwegian bokmål, simple words

## Skip

Scary or sad turns, villains who win, death, hospitals, screens, brands, real news, morals that say "you should", sugar and sweets as rewards, cliffhangers.

## Sources, check first

None. Do not search or fetch. Write the story yourself.

## This week

(Parent edits: "Mina lost her first tooth", "visit to grandma on Saturday")

## Each story must state

- A title, then the story. No date line, no sources.
- A last line of one soft sentence about sleep

## No change

Always write a new story. Never reuse a title, a main animal or the repeated line from the last 14 stories.

## Language and place

Write Norwegian bokmål. Norwegian setting (forest, fjord, cabin), Europe/Oslo.
```

## Sample output (hand-written, 23 Nov 2026; not a test run)

**Pelle og den lille dinosauren som ikke fant sengen sin**

Det var en gang, i skogen bak huset, en liten dinosaur som het Tuss. Tuss var ikke større enn
en katt. Da kvelden kom, ville alle dyrene i skogen sove. Men Tuss fant ikke sengen sin.

«Jeg skal hjelpe deg,» sa Pelle, kaninen. «Vi går sakte, sakte.» Og så gikk de, steg for steg,
og Pelle sa: «Sakte, sakte, sakte.»

De kom til et gammelt tre. Ugla satt på en grein. «Sengen din,» hvisket ugla, «er der det
er mykest.» Tuss kjente på mose. Den var kald. Han kjente på bar jord. Den var hard. Han kjente
på en haug med gamle blader, og den var myk og varm. «Her!» sa Tuss.

Men en eneste ting manglet. Tuss kunne ikke sove uten en bamse. Pelle tenkte seg om. Så ga
han Tuss øret sitt å holde i, bare litt, bare i kveld. «Sakte, sakte, sakte,» sa Pelle.

Tuss lukket øynene. Ett. To. Tre. Fire. Fem. Seks. Syv. Og det var akkurat syv stjerner
på himmelen den kvelden, for Mina hadde talt dem.

Skogen ble stille. Pelle gikk hjem, sakte, sakte, og krøp ned under dynen sin.
Og nå sover alle, du også.

_(about 190 words here to show the shape; a real run writes about 400.)_

## Checks for a make-mode brief (instead of the six-point checklist)

1. New: title, animal and repeated line differ from the last 14 stories.
2. Fits the doc: name, age, interests, length, no Skip item.
3. Calm ending, no scares.
4. Language: natural bokmål, short sentences, no English calques. Have a Norwegian reader check
   the first week of stories.
5. Safe: no real brands, no real people beyond the family names in the doc.
6. Never claims facts about the real world as news.
