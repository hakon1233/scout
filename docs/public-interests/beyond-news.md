# Beyond news: topics people want every day or week

Scout's topics so far are news: what changed, with dated stories and sources. A topic can
also be a thing someone wants delivered on a rhythm: a verse, a story, a lesson, a plan. This
file lists 50 such niches, picks starters, and says what the run system would need. Research
on 23 Nov 2026 (web search and fetch only; no API). "Pays today" quotes only what a search
found; "not checked" means no figure was found.

## What makes a good recurring topic that is not news

1. **A rhythm the reader already has.** Bedtime, the Sunday walk, payday, Advent, the school
   term. The brief rides a habit instead of making a new one.
2. **One small thing, done well.** One verse, one story, one word, one challenge. Not a digest.
3. **Personal enough that a generic app is wrong.** A child's name, a level, a region, a diet.
   That is what an intent doc is for, and what a fixed feed cannot do.
4. **Fresh by the calendar, not by the news.** Church year, season, weather, school term. If a
   dated page exists (bibel.no's verse of the day), use it; if not, the date alone is the input.
5. **Does not repeat, and may build.** The reader notices a repeated verse or story at once.
   A series (day 7 of 30) is worth more than 30 separate items.
6. **Safe to write.** No medical advice, no money advice, no text we may not copy. Check the
   licence of any text before it goes into a brief (see "Licences").
7. **Calm ending.** Reading should take under 3 minutes and leave nothing to do but the one thing.
8. **Someone already pays for the nearest app.** Duolingo (12.2M paid), Hallow (about $40M in
   2025), kids' story apps ($30-50 a year). Scout's edge is the personal doc and Norway.

## Niches

Fit codes: **Yes** = works with today's run (web sources, dated). **Mem** = needs memory of
what was sent. **Make** = needs a mode with no web sources. **Series** = needs a position in
a plan. **Time** = needs a delivery hour or a date countdown. **Pr** = needs the reader's
details (names, level) kept in the doc.

## Starter topics

Eleven that could launch, with a one-line reason each. The first five have an intent doc in
`intent-docs/`.

1. **Dagens bibelord** (`dagens-bibelord-norway.md`, tested, works today). The source does the
   picking; free to link; fast to launch.
2. **Bedtime story for my kids** (`bedtime-story-for-my-kids.md`). Parents already pay for
   apps; the intent doc is the product.
3. **Poem or quote of the day** (`dikt-og-sitat-av-dagen.md`). Daily, universal, cheap; needs
   sent-list memory.
4. **Norwegian word of the day** (`norsk-ord-for-dagen.md`). Big paying market; Scout's Norway
   edge; sits next to `norwegian-test-dates.md`.
5. **Frost and garden jobs, Trøndelag** (`frost-garden-trondelag.md`, tested 4/6). Works today
   as a weather verdict; shows what "make" mode adds.
6. **Advent calendar for our family.** Advent starts 29 Nov 2026; 24 days, a clear start and end.
7. **Weekend outing with the weather verdict.** Fits today; the Friday habit is strong.
8. **Week's dinner plan from the season.** The most daily need on the list; needs memory.
9. **30-day challenge** (first 5 km or similar). The series idea in its simplest form.
10. **Question for the two of us.** Couples' apps already charge; tiny to build.
11. **Holiday and bridge-day planner, Norway.** Fits today; useful four times a year.

## Licences

- **Bibelselskapet** (bibel.no/verbum/rettigheter-til-bruk-av-bibeltekst, 23 Nov 2026). Bibel
  2011 and 2024, NO78/85, Studiebibelen and the New Testament 2005 are copyrighted. Allowed
  without asking: private use, quotation up to 5 verses, "Dagens bibelord" on a website
  (but a website or an app otherwise needs permission and usually a fee). Not allowed: a text
  used as a digital Bible or in large parts, changes to the text, AI-chosen verses or AI
  answers to theological questions, AI-generated music or speech, and Bible text combined with
  AI-generated text or pictures. **Public domain:** the 1930 bokmål and 1938 nynorsk translations
  and Bibal 1895 (North Sami). **Our choice:** reference and link only; ask Bibelselskapet before
  printing text; or use the 1930 text.
- **Quotes and poems.** Norwegian copyright lasts 70 years after the author's death. Ibsen,
  Bjørnson, Wergeland and Welhaven are free. Wikiquote is CC BY-SA 4.0 (credit and link). Never
  copy a quote collection; the model writes its own framing and cites the source work.
- **Dictionaries.** Link to Bokmålsordboka and Store norske leksikon; write your own
  definitions and examples.
- **Stories.** A child's story is written fresh; no characters from books or films we do not own.
- **English Bibles.** King James and World English Bible are public domain; ESV, NIV and NLT are not.

## Run-system changes needed (described only)

Ranked by how many starters need them.

1. **A "make" mode.** The topic is flagged as "write, don't research": no searches or fetches,
   no source links or dates required, one long item allowed (400-600 words), the freshness
   check off. Needed by 7 of 11 starters (bedtime, advent, dinner, couples, quote, word, challenge).
2. **Memory of what was sent.** A small per-interest list of keys (verse reference, quote's first
   words, story title and animal, word) passed to the next run as "do not repeat". The
   existing proposal feeds the last 2 briefs; this one needs 14-90 days or a plain list.
3. **A position in a series.** A counter ("day 7 of 30") and the plan's outline so the model keeps
   the arc; the brief states the day. Needed by advent, challenge, Lent, countdowns.
4. **Reader details in the doc, kept private.** Names, ages and level go into the intent doc;
   the doc must never become public with them. A "private details" block that public sharing strips.
5. **Delivery time and date anchors.** A time of day (19:30 for a bedtime story, 06:30 for a
   verse), and "start on 1 Dec / ends on 24 Dec". Today a run is daily or weekly.
6. **An evergreen flag per interest** (already in the chief's list), so nostalgia and word
   topics are not cut by the 30-day freshness check.
7. **A "date is the input" source.** Pass today's date, weekday, church-year season, holidays
   and the weather summary to the run as context so make-mode topics can be seasonal.
8. **Reader feedback ("more like this", "too long").** A thumb stored with the interest and added
   to the next run; optional, after the first six.
9. **Content rules per format.** Kids' stories: no scares, no brands. Bible text: no text, no
   interpretation unless Bibelselskapet agrees. Quotes: check the wording against a fetched page.
10. **Test rubric for make-mode.** The six-point checklist assumes sources. Use the make-mode
    checks in `bedtime-story-for-my-kids.md` until the team settles one.
