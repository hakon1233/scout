## Source notes

- **Best dated feeds:** regjeringen.no (dated headlines) and handball.no.
- **Readable but undated:** luftfartstilsynet, fiskeridir, husbanken, forskerforum,
  forbrukerradet. Scout can read them but can't tell what is new; pair each with a dated
  source.
- **Hard to read:** nav.no rule pages (JavaScript), doffin.no (empty shell), parts of
  skatteetaten.no and udir.no (404 on the paths tried).

## Dropped

Board games, cosplay and 3D printing in Norway (no single public source, only shops and
forums); The Gathering (once a year); ski-track status per region (too thin on its own).
