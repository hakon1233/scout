# Naming: replacing "Scout"

Checked 23 Nov 2026. Domain status comes from RDAP at the registries (Verisign for .com, Google for .app, Identity Digital for .ai, Norid for .no). "Free" means RDAP returned 404, so likely registrable; confirm at a registrar before buying. App and company clashes come from web search only. No trademark register was queried directly (EUIPO, USPTO and Patentstyret are not indexed by search), so every finalist needs a real search by a person or a trademark attorney before launch.

## Verdict on Scout: drop it

- **Domains.** scout.com, scout.app, scout.ai and scout.no are all registered. getscout.com, getscout.app, getscout.ai, usescout.com, usescout.app and scoutbrief.com/.app/.ai are all registered too. Only the `.no` versions of getscout, usescout and scoutbrief are free.
- **Direct clash.** Yutori sells "Scouts", always-on AI agents that watch the web and send alerts (iOS app launched March 2026, paid plans, Product Hunt, Fortune coverage). That is our product category and our product word. Open Scouts (Firecrawl, open source) does the same. Scout APM and ScoutITai are monitoring tools. Scout Alarm is home security.
- **Searchability.** Poor. "Scout" returns Scouting, Scout APM, Yutori and Scout Alarm.
- **Fit.** It says "finds things", not "delivers a calm brief every morning". It does not cover a Bible verse or a bedtime story.

## Method

- Wrote about 250 candidates across messengers, watchers, rhythms, Norse and nature words, invented words, verbs, two-word names and Norwegian compounds.
- Checked .com, .app, .ai and .no for all of them by script.
- Finding: **every good single word is taken** on all four extensions (courier, herald, lookout, beacon, varde, cairn, almanac, daymark, huginn, rookery, and so on). What is free are compounds and Norwegian words.
- Shortlisted 15 for meaning, sound in both languages and spelling after hearing. Searched the web for apps and companies with the same or a similar name.
- Also found: AI morning-brief apps are a crowded niche (Briefling, DayStart AI, Prism, The Brief, Aureate, Previously On AI, Signal News). Names built on "brief", "morning", "dawn" or "daily" blur into them.
- Public registrar prices (Porkbun, 23 Nov): .com $11.81/yr, .app $14.93/yr renewal, .ai $82.70/yr, .no not listed there (usually about NOK 150-200/yr, check Domeneshop or Norid registrars).

## Top 3

1. **Lamplighter. Buy `lamplighter.app`** (about $15/yr). Add `lamplighter.no` too. Why: a real English word, picture-friendly, easy to say in Norwegian and English, and it fits every use (news, a verse, a bedtime story, "all quiet tonight"). No clash found in AI briefing. Weakness: .com is taken, so lead with the .app. If you want a .com, `lamplighterapp.com` is free.
2. **Almanakk. Buy `almanakk.com`** (about $12/yr). Add `almanakk.app` and `almanakk.ai` if you want to defend it. Why: the cleanest domain set, a rhythm word that covers non-news, Norwegian-first and still guessable. Weakness: English speakers spell it "almanac" and will land on almanac.com.
3. **Ravnpost. Buy `ravnpost.com`** (about $12/yr), plus `ravnpost.no` and `.app`. Why: the Odin's-ravens story fits the product and no one else has it. Weakness: "Ravn" is hard for English speakers. Pick this if the Norse story matters more than easy spelling.

Varde is the strongest meaning but is not recommended: its domains are gone and Norway already has a Varde news paper and three Varde companies.
